# GridPulse model card

## Intended use

Educational demonstration of a supervised machine-learning workflow for next-hour electricity-demand spike classification. Not for operational grid management.

## Data

- 8,760 hourly synthetic records for 2025.
- Fixed random seed: 42.
- Target: next-hour demand exceeds 110% of the previous 24-hour mean.
- Predictors: hour, day of week, weekend flag, temperature, humidity, current demand and previous-hour demand.

## Evaluation design

Chronological split: 70% training, 10% validation and 20% untouched test. The decision threshold is selected on validation data, not the test set. The same chronological test period is used for baseline and candidate model comparisons.

## Selected model and test results

- Selected model: Random Forest.
- Decision threshold: 0.445.
- Accuracy: 0.9532.
- Precision: 0.6686.
- Recall: 0.8298.
- F1-score: 0.7405.
- Confusion matrix (rows = actual Normal/Spike, columns = predicted Normal/Spike): `[[1553, 58], [24, 117]]`.

The authoritative full comparison, validation scores and subgroup metrics are stored in `outputs/metrics.json` and are regenerated by `python train_model.py`.

## Limitations and risks

The high-level performance is measured on synthetic data whose patterns are controlled by the generator. Real-world performance may be substantially lower. The probability is a model estimate, not a calibrated operational risk forecast. A change in input can change the probability without necessarily changing the final binary alert if the probability remains on the same side of the decision threshold.
